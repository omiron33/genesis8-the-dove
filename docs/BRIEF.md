# Genesis 8, The Dove: director brief

**Title:** The Waterline. **Mode:** code-only, GPU photoreal mode, 1920×1080 at 60 fps.
**Direction:** the craft, depth and modernity of a code-drawn motion piece rather than literal photorealism, built on the scale and light of the approved Ararat test shot.

## Design system

- **Running motif:** the waterline. One thin line of gold light is the level of the flood. It is
  in every scene, physically: the sea's surface, a tide mark on Ararat, the ark's window sill, the
  dove's horizon, the edge of the drying ground, the altar's ember line, and finally the horizon of
  the earth that will not be cursed again. It falls through the film and, at the end, settles as the
  horizon. A small gauge travels with it: day count and level, in the annotation voice.
- **Palette and colour build:** flood slate and silver (#1c242c to #9aa6b0) with bone-white type
  for the first third. The first warmth enters with the dove (apricot, #f0b089). From "Come out of
  the ark" the world is in full colour: olive and young green (#7f8f3a, #a8c060), earth ochre and
  harvest gold (#d6a34a). **Signal colour:** waterline gold (#ffcc70). Only the signal glows.
- **Type:** EB Garamond is the lyric voice (roman for narration, italic for asides and quiet
  lines). Inter Tight, tracked capitals, is the gauge and annotation voice (chapter and verse, day
  counts, dates). The sacred register ("The Lord God", God's words) is EB Garamond small capitals,
  widely spaced, set larger and slower. Typographic punctuation throughout.
- **Finish:** the engine's shared film finish (sub-frame motion blur, bloom only from the signal
  and sun, ACES, grain, vignette).
- **No faces, no people drawn realistically.** Noah is present as gesture and silhouette only (a
  hand at the window, far figures on a path). Birds and animals are sculpted forms (folded,
  faceted surfaces), elegant rather than cute.
