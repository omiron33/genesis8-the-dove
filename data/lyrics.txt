[Intro]
[Verse 1]
God remembered Noe,
and every beast and bird,
every herd and creeping thing
that shared the ark with him.
God sent a wind across the earth;
the waters began to fall.
The springs of the deep were closed,
heaven’s floodgates shut;
the rain from heaven was held back.
The waters ebbed from all the earth.
After one hundred fifty days
they had fallen; the ark came to rest,
in month seven, day twenty-seven,
on Ararat’s mountains.
The waters kept on falling
until the tenth month.
On the first day of month ten,
the mountain peaks came into view.
After forty days, Noe opened
the window he had made in the ark.
He sent a raven out;
it flew until the earth was dry,
and did not come back.
Then he sent a dove to learn
if waters had withdrawn from earth.
The dove found no place to rest;
water still covered all the earth.
She came back to Noe in the ark.
He reached and drew her in,
held her close inside.
He waited seven more days,
then sent the dove out again.
At evening she came back,
an olive leaf held in her beak.
Noe knew the waters had withdrawn.
He waited seven more days,
then sent the dove out once more.
She did not return again.
In Noe’s six hundred first year,
first month, the first day,
the waters had ebbed from earth.
Noe opened the ark’s covering
and saw the ground was dry.
In month two, on day twenty-seven,
the earth was dry.
The Lord God spoke to Noe:

[Verse 2]
[Climax]
“Come out of the ark,
you, your wife, your sons,
and your sons’ wives with you.
Bring out every creature with you:
birds, beasts, all living flesh,
everything that crawls on earth.
Let them teem and multiply;
let them fill the earth.”
Noe came out with his wife,
his sons and their wives.
Every beast and herd,
every bird and creeping thing,
each by its kind,
came out of the ark.
Noe built an altar to the Lord.
From every clean beast and bird
he offered whole burnt offerings.
The Lord God received the pleasing scent
and said within himself:
“I will not curse the earth again
for what humanity has done.
The human heart leans toward evil
even from youth.
I will not strike down all life again
as I have done.”

[Outro]
All earth’s days shall keep their rhythm:
seedtime, harvest, cold and heat,
summer, spring, day and night—
none shall cease.”
